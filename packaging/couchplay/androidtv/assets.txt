CouchPlay Android TV / Google TV assets

couchplay-icon-1280x720.png: 16:9 artwork with the original sofa at 75% of the canvas width.
couchplay-banner-1280x720.png: banner master with the CouchPlay name included.
couchplay-banner-640x360.png: higher-density banner export.
couchplay-banner-320x180.png: xhdpi launcher banner, ready to copy as drawable-xhdpi/couchplay_banner.png and reference with android:banner="@drawable/couchplay_banner".

All images are opaque RGB PNGs with the original vertical grey gradient and charcoal glyph.
The sofa is scaled uniformly from ../glyph.png, with its original proportions preserved.
The banner name uses the project's Inter SemiBold font; its licence is in notices/Inter-OFL.txt.
Regenerate all CouchPlay assets with: python packaging/couchplay/export_icons.py

Android TV requires both a square launcher icon and a 16:9 banner containing the app name.
The existing ../couchplay-square-1024.png remains available for the square icon.
Guidance checked on 2026-10-22:
https://developer.android.com/design/ui/tv/guides/system/tv-app-icon-guidelines
https://developer.android.com/training/tv/get-started/create
